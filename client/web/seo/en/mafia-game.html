Mafia game (text-based) in your browser

The Mob State is a mafia game you can play in the browser (PWA). The UI is text-based (menus + timers) so it loads fast, plays well on mobile, and works great in short sessions.

What you can do in-game (high level):

  • Commit crimes, work jobs, and grow your account with XP and money.
  • Run vehicle operations and expand your vehicle portfolio over time.
  • Claim daily/weekly goals for small rewards and steady motivation.
  • Play seasons/events and climb leaderboards with your crew.

Frequently asked questions

What is The Mob State?

A browser mafia game with long-term progression: you build power through money, items, and social systems like crews, while the world runs on timers and competitive seasons.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Use your mobile web browser, or install the PWA to your home screen for faster access.
