Frequently asked questions

What is a text based mafia game?

It is a mafia / crime strategy game that uses a text-based UI: menus, timers, and narrative-style feedback rather than 3D action. Progression is the core loop: money, XP, vehicles, crew, and long-term goals.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Use your mobile browser or install the PWA. The same account works across devices (via your session/login).

Is it free to start?

Yes—core play is free to start. Optional premium can exist, but the core experience remains playable without paying.
